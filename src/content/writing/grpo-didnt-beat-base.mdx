## The pipeline

<Flow steps={['Qwen 7B base', 'SFT', '*GRPO', 'eval · BIRD dev']} caption="Each stage starts from the previous checkpoint." />

GRPO scores each sampled answer relative to the others in its group:

$$
A_i = \frac{r_i - \operatorname{mean}(r)}{\operatorname{std}(r)}
$$

<Callout>If every sample in a group gets the same reward, every advantage is zero and that group teaches the model nothing.</Callout>

## Why voting works

Self-consistency samples several SQL candidates, executes each one, and keeps the result that shows up most often.

